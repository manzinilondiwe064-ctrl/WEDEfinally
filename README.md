About Our New Fantastic Website
Bella's Bakes is a warm, family bakery from Soweto. Our new website shows the love we put into our cakes, breads and cupcakes. We designed it to be welcoming like our bakery when you open it, you feel at home.

This is NOT the old website. This is Part 2, completely new and improved! Everything works perfectly now. The colours are beautiful warm brown and cream like chocolate and flour. The pictures of our cakes are clear and mouth-watering!

Why Our Website is Fantastic
We worked very hard to make this website fantastic:

1.Anyone can use it! No confusion. The menu is at the top - Home, About, Products, Enquiry, Contact. Just click and you go there. Even my gogo can use it on her phone
2.Our cakes, breads and cupcakes are shown in beautiful cards with prices. You can see everything clearly before you order.
3. On the Enquiry page, customers can quickly fill in what cake they want, their name and number. It is simple and fast, no difficult steps.
5. On the About page we share our family bakery story, why we love baking. Customers feel connected to us.

 What You Can Do On The Website
- Home: See our welcome message and our best-selling cakes.
- About: Read about our bakery family and our values.
- Products:Browse all our delicious cakes and breads with prices. All pictures load fast!
- Enquiry: Want a birthday cake? Just fill the simple form - we will call you back.
- Contact: Find our hours, location, and send us a message anytime.

It is that simple! No difficult instructions needed.

I Put Lot of Work Into It
This website was built with love from scratch. We have 5 pages, all linked together perfectly. We have one beautiful stylesheet that makes everything match. We have an images folder with all our cakes. We tested it many times to make sure every button works.
