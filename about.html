Our Story • Our Mission • Our Vision • Our Goals

Hi, I'm Bella Khumalo ❤️

Started in mom's small kitchen in Soweto during lockdown 2021 with R500, one broken oven, and a dream. My gogo taught me to bake when I was 12. Today: 5 women team, 1,200+ clients, voted Best Home Baker Gauteng 2024.

Why Bella's Bakes? Because every birthday, wedding, graduation deserves a cake that looks as good as the memory feels.

🎯

OUR MISSION

To transform ordinary moments into golden unforgettable memories through exceptional handcrafted bakes while empowering young township women to become bakers.

👁️

OUR VISION

To become Africa's most loved luxury home bakery by 2030 - where Bella's is the first name when celebrating love, success, and life in every African home.

🏆

OUR GOAL 2026-2027

Open boutique in Sandton City, launch Bella's Baking Academy to train 100 girls yearly, and start same-day delivery app. Profit with purpose.

Our Values - What We Stand For

🌟 Quality Over Quantity

We bake ONLY 10 cakes/day. Perfection cannot be rushed. 3 hours decoration minimum.

🤎 Ubuntu

10% of every sale feeds kids at Diepsloot Primary. Your cake feeds a child.

💛 Gold Standard Service

Free handwritten card, free Jozi delivery over R500, emergency 2AM cake? We got you.
